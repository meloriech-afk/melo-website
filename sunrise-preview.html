Our Story

Your neighborhood cafe on Golden Street

Sunrise Cafe is a neighborhood coffee spot in the heart of downtown New London, on Golden Street — the kind of place where the morning starts slow, the coffee is fresh, and nobody is in a hurry to rush you out the door.

We keep things simple on purpose: good coffee made to order, pastries and sweets ready in the case, and a warm welcome whether you’re grabbing a cup on your way to work or settling in with a friend.

Stop by, say hello, and make us part of your morning routine.

  • Made to orderEvery coffee is brewed and built when you ask for it — never sitting around.
  • Part of the neighborhoodWe’re proud to be a small, local stop in downtown New London.
  • Easy & welcomingWalk-ins, regulars, and first-timers all get the same warm hello.
