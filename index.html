Behind the employee is MELO’s Task-to-Completion Engine — the system that turns a plain-language request into finished, verified work.

Questions

Before you ask

What exactly happens with the free first job?

We agree on one small, defined piece of work — for example, a landing page, a bug fix, or one automation. The AI Software Engineer builds and tests it fast — we aim for 24–48 hours — and you receive the finished work with a delivery report. It’s free, needs no payment details, and carries no obligation to continue.

Is this a real product, or a concept?

MELO Workforce is a new product, and this page is honest about that: we are onboarding our first pilot customers now, starting with the AI Software Engineer. That’s exactly why the free first job exists — you judge real delivered work before paying anything, instead of trusting claims.

What can the Software Engineer actually build?

Full-stack websites and web applications, the backends, APIs and databases behind them, automations and integrations between your tools, plus ongoing bug fixes and maintenance. Work is built from an agreed specification, tested, and prepared for deployment — with your approval before it goes live.

Will it change my live website without telling me?

No. Production deployments and other consequential actions require your explicit approval first. You also receive delivery reports that separate what was completed from what is pending or unverified.

How do I pay?

By check, monthly — currently the only payment method MELO accepts. Make checks payable to Melokuhle Msweli and mail them to 27 Masonic St Ste 1, New London, CT 06320. No card is kept on file and nothing is charged automatically. Access stays active while the monthly payment is current — if a month's check hasn't been received, the service locks until payment arrives.

What if I want more than the Software Engineer later?

Move up a plan. MELO Business gives you any three coordinated MELO AI employees for $2,000/mo, and MELO Full Workforce gives you all five — Executive Operator, Revenue Recovery, Customer Resolution, Procurement, and Software Engineer — plus the full Task-to-Completion Engine, for $3,000/mo.
