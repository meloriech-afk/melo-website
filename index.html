Honest answers

Is MELO an established company with lots of clients?

No — and we'd rather say so. MELO is new and onboarding its first clients now, which is exactly why the $5,000 website is built and shown to you before you pay. You judge finished work, not promises or a reputation we haven't earned yet.

Who owns my website?

You do. Once your check is received, the finished website is launched on your own domain and hosting and it's fully yours. If you later take the Engineer plan and then cancel it, your website still stays yours — what stops is the ongoing engineer service and its live features, not your site.

Do you host my website?

No. Your website lives on your own hosting, under your control. MELO is the engineer: with your permission, we connect and change the code — you always see changes in a preview and approve before anything goes live.

How do you connect to my website?

One simple connect step, matched to what you already have: sign in with GitHub if your site lives there, install our small plugin on WordPress, add us as an editor on Wix/Squarespace, or grant hosting access. You never type a password into MELO, and you can disconnect anytime.

Will a new website guarantee me more customers?

No honest builder can guarantee that. What we guarantee is the work itself: a professional, fast, trustworthy website with your reviews up front and a clear path for visitors to call or request a quote — built and shown to you before you pay.

Why only checks?

Simple and safe for both sides while we're starting out. No card details ever float around in emails or texts. Direct transfer may be added later; we'll tell clients directly when it is.
